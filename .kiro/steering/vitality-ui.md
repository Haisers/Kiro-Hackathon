# Vitality UI product guidance

Apply these rules to Vitality UI work in this repository:

- Provide only two staff views: **BHW** and **Hospital team**. Do not add a patient-facing portal or patient role switcher.
- Design for people with low technical confidence. Use short, familiar words and explain one action at a time.
- Avoid technical terms, IDs, storage details, AI implementation details, and system language in primary UI labels. Put essential safety notes in plain language.
- Keep screens compact and task-focused: restrained spacing, fewer nested cards, modest headings, and short descriptions.
- Preserve at least 44px touch targets, 16px mobile form text, clear back buttons, readable contrast, visible status text, and helpful validation.
- Do not confuse a referral with a confirmed appointment. Keep attendance separate from referral status.
- Keep all demo-data, mock-upload, AI-draft, and clinical-boundary disclosures accurate but concise.
